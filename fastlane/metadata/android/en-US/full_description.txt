Acuranza is a private, fully offline personal finance tracker for Android, built for Indian banking.

It reads bank SMS (if you allow it), turning them into transactions, balances and card bills automatically —
all on your phone. No account, no servers, no analytics: the app does not even request the INTERNET
permission, so Android itself guarantees your data cannot leave the device.

• Bank SMS import with a review inbox: confirm or fix anything it reads
• Accounts, credit cards, balances that reconcile with what your bank says
• Budgets that follow your salary cycle, with rollover and an envelope mode
• Goals with Monte-Carlo success probability, and safe-to-spend until payday
• Recurring payments, subscriptions, loans and EMIs, card-bill reminders
• Insights computed offline: spending spikes, savings rate, card utilisation
• Receipts and warranties, PDF reports, CSV/Beancount/Ledger export
• Encrypted database (SQLCipher + Android Keystore), biometric lock, decoy PIN, encrypted backups

Acuranza is free software under GPL-3.0-or-later, based on the Kosh project by Amit Kumar Jha and
contributors (github.com/AIM-IT4/kosh), with a redesigned interface, a strict offline build and a
notification gate on first launch.

Notifications are required at first launch — that is how bill due dates, spend alerts and warranty
reminders reach you. Nothing else is asked for up front; SMS access is optional.
