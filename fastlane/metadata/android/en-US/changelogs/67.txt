Widget redesign inspired by fitness-ring and screen-time widgets: This Month leads with your net savings beside a progress ring (budget progress, or savings rate when no budget is set); Savings Goal gets a ring, headline figure and remaining-to-go; a new Spending by Category widget shows a donut of the period's top categories with a legend; Quick Add tiles now carry icons. Tapping a widget opens its own page - This Month to Analytics, Savings Goal to a new goal screen where you can set or clear the target (it no longer just opens the app). Layouts are top-aligned and sized so small widget sizes clip the caption, never the numbers.
