Widgets, reworked: the Net Worth, Recent Transactions and Upcoming widgets are removed. Quick Add is now a balanced 2x2 grid (expense, income, transfer, transactions) that never clips on small sizes. This Month is redesigned - income, spending, net savings (negative turns red), a savings-rate line, and budget progress with a caption. New: a Savings Goal widget (progress toward your configured target, with an empty state) and a Shortcuts widget (bills, budgets, reports, accounts). Also from the audit pass: the in-app installer no longer mis-rejects on Android 8.x, a static-callback leak is fixed, and Lint runs clean.
