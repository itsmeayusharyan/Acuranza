NEW: automatic SMS capture, permissionless. Enable it under Settings → Financial tools → SMS transaction tracking: Acuranza reads the Messages app's notifications as bank SMS arrive and parks them for your review — same pipeline, same review, nothing saved until you confirm. It uses Notification Access (no SMS permission, so no Play Protect conflict), only ever looks at the SMS app's notifications, and discards OTPs, promotions, personal chats and anything ambiguous. Off by default; turning it off stops capture instantly. Android 13+ sideloaded installs: allow restricted settings first (App info ⋮), then grant notification access.
