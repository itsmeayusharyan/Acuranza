NEW: SMS transaction tracking (Settings → Financial tools), fully on-device. With your explicit consent and the SMS permission, Acuranza reads bank and payment messages, extracts amount, date, merchant and account, and imports them as transactions you confirm first. Message bodies are never stored. Automatic background scanning about every 6 hours (switchable, survives reboot, stops gracefully if permission is revoked), manual refresh, batch undo, per-import delete and a one-tap delete of everything SMS-imported. Note: sideloaded installs may see a Play Protect warning because of the SMS permission.
