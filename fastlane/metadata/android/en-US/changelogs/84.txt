Edit navigation tabs (Settings > Appearance): a visual customizer for the bottom bar with a live preview of the real thing. Rename tabs and pick icons (display-only - destinations never change), reorder, hide tabs you don't use (at least two stay visible; a hidden landing tab moves the landing to the first visible one), show or hide labels, and switch between the floating pill and a docked bar. Save/Cancel with an unsaved-changes warning, and Reset returns the original four tabs.
