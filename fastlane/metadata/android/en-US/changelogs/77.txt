First launch is shorter and friendlier: the bank-account and credit-card setup screens are gone from onboarding — nobody is asked to add accounts or cards before using the app. The flow is now Welcome (redesigned around what Acuranza does: income, expenses, budgets and goals, all on your phone), the required notification step, an optional import step, and done. Accounts and cards are added any time from the Accounts screen; existing users are unaffected and never see onboarding again.
