Haptic feedback is now a proper control panel (Settings > Appearance > Haptic feedback): a master switch, a Light/Standard/Strong strength choice with a Test button, and a separate preference for the calculator keypad, saves and confirmations, and tabs and buttons. Turning the master switch off silences everything but keeps your choices; the keypad also now respects the master switch (it previously vibrated even with haptics off). Android's own touch-feedback setting still has the final word on every device.
