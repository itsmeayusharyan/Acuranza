Home-screen widgets: three new widgets join This month and Quick add — Balances (net worth + accounts, cards show what's owed), Recent transactions (latest four, tap a row for its detail) and Upcoming payments (unpaid bills with overdue first, plus recurring due within a week). Quick add gained Transfer and Transactions buttons. All widgets follow your light/dark theme, hide amounts under privacy mode or app lock (unless you opted in), and refresh automatically after transactions, bills and recurring changes. Everything stays on-device.
