SMS tracking, re-architected to need no SMS permission: Play Protect was blocking installs of v1.10.0 because it requested READ_SMS. Bank messages now arrive only when you share them from your Messages app to Acuranza or paste them in Settings → Financial tools; OTP/PIN/password messages, promotions, balance alerts and ambiguous text are never processed, message text is parsed and discarded, and imports still go through your explicit review with per-item editing, batch undo and one-tap delete of all SMS data. No permission change otherwise; fully offline.
