One-tap SMS capture: copy a bank message in your Messages app, open SMS transaction tracking, and Acuranza offers to parse it with a single tap (the clipboard is cleared afterwards). The clipboard is read only while this screen is open, the same pipeline rejects OTPs, promotions and personal chats, and nothing is stored until you confirm. No permissions added — the automatic listener from v1.13.0 stays removed because Play Protect blocks sideloaded apps that use it.
