Play Protect install fix: v1.13.0's automatic capture read bank SMS through notification access, and Play Protect flags sideloaded apps that declare a notification listener the same way it flags SMS permissions — the install was blocked. The listener is removed; the manifest is back to the v1.12.0 shape that installs cleanly. Bank messages still arrive by the share sheet or paste, with the same parsing, review, dedup and undo. Automatic inbox/notification capture can only ship through Play Store distribution, which Acuranza does not use.
