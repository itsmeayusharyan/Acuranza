SMS tracking consent workflow: withdrawing consent now opens a clear confirmation of exactly what will be deleted, then stops all scanning and notification capture immediately, deletes every pending candidate and SMS-imported transaction (your own entries, CSV imports, budgets and balances are untouched), and points you to system settings to revoke the SMS permission, since Android doesn't let an app revoke it itself. Enabling tracking again shows the consent screen, then requests the SMS permission right away (full build); if it's denied, tracking stays off with instructions for granting it in system settings.
