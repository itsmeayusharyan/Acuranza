Slice bank support: messages from Slice (SLCEIT headers) are now identified as Slice and parsed properly — UPI credits and debits carry the masked account, payee and reference ID (including the 'Ref ID:' phrasing), credit-card repayments file as card payments on the Slice card rather than salary or spending, and balance updates stay balance metadata. Fingerprint compatibility means re-scans never double-import older Slice messages.
